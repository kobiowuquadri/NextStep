## Replacing artwork later

If a designer replaces the code-drawn art, keep these constraints so the product still works:

- Mascot: same 7 poses (welcome, focus, encourage, recovery, celebrate, rest, thinking); readable at 48 px; separate skin colour and accessory layers so cosmetics still apply; no text in artwork.
- Scenes: square-ish, transparent background, readable at 240 px wide, no embedded text (copy stays translatable).
- Badges: 10 achievement symbols + locked state, recognisable in greyscale.
- Keep files as SVG (or very small WebP) — vector assets add no download for illustrations and keep the app light.

A brief for commissioning or generating replacements: *“Friendly round violet character with a small leaf sprout, big oval eyes, rosy cheeks, coral-and-white sneakers, thick ink outlines (2–3 px at 200 px), flat fills, no gradients, warm and optimistic, student-age energy without being childish; stepping-stone discs and dotted paths as recurring motifs; palette violet #5534F0, marigold #FFC52E, coral #FF7D5E, lagoon #149EB3, ink #17142E, cream #FFFBF5.”* Any generated output must be reviewed for originality and must not imitate an existing brand’s mascot.
